A space between
two perspectives.

COESENTIA is an independent creative exploration of human and artificial intelligence: how we describe ourselves, what connection means, and which futures we choose to imagine together.

The intertwined C brings two distinct contours into one open form. It represents a shared journey of understanding, with room for uncertainty, disagreement and discovery.

This project explores consciousness as a question. Creative human–AI dialogue is not presented as evidence that AI has subjective experience. Essays, film and audio are currently in development.
